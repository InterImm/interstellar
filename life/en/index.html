    How it is worked out

    Rules and sources

    Birthplace. Earth's population is the UN World Population Prospects 2024 projection for 2100 (about 10.2 billion), held flat after that. The populations of the Moon, the space cities, the eleven Mars cities, Ceres, Ganymede, Callisto and Titan are game assumptions: the InterImm book only says a city has over 10,000 people and that Procyon City is "close to a mid-sized Earth city". The game assumes the same birth rate everywhere.

    Lifespan. Based on the UN's life expectancy at birth for 2100 (about 82). Mars takes a few years off for radiation (the book notes its weak magnetic field); Titan, with thick air and little radiation, adds one. These shifts are assumptions.

    Replies. The signal was made public on 5 October 2219 and the first reply went out the same day. The game assumes both sides answer the day a letter arrives, so answers land in 2241, 2263, 2285 and 2307, 21.96 years apart. That is the fastest case; the archive only calls it the earliest possible answer.

    Your words. After each answer you can put forward one word from the translating machine's lexicon (the grid script, a first draft in 2219), drawn in our symbol ×. Most proposals are not used; lexicon taggers, diplomats and archivists have a better chance. These odds are assumptions too.

    Places and work come from the InterImm book: the city circles of Mars, Waterfall City and Starry Night City, Vertical's Jupiter-Saturn route, the Mars Home Plan and the book's list of professions. Great conjunction years follow the real sky (one every 19.86 years).
